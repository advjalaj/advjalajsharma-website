Areas of practice

What the practice covers

Matters are handled from drafting through final arguments, at the trial stage and in appeals and revisions.

01

Criminal trials & defence

Defence and prosecution of criminal cases under the BNS, BNSS and special statutes, from charge through final arguments.

02

Bail & quashing

Regular and anticipatory bail, cancellation of bail, and petitions before the Delhi High Court for quashing of FIRs and proceedings.

03

Criminal complaints & investigation

Private complaints and applications under Section 175(3) and Section 223 BNSS, police complaints, and applications for monitoring of investigation.

04

Cheque dishonour

Statutory notices, complaints and defence under Section 138 of the Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881.

05

Matrimonial & family

Divorce, including divorce by mutual consent, and related family and matrimonial proceedings.

06

Property, partition & tenancy

Partition suits, eviction, injunctions and interim relief under the CPC, and first appeals arising from them.

07

Consumer disputes

Complaints and appeals before District and State Consumer Commissions involving products, services and builders.

08

Real estate & builder disputes

Claims against developers for possession, conveyance and refunds, including proceedings involving RERA.

09

Recovery & arbitration

Money recovery suits, commercial disputes and arbitration proceedings.

10

Motor accident claims

Claim petitions for compensation before Motor Accident Claims Tribunals.

About

Jalaj Sharma

Jalaj Sharma is an advocate practising civil and criminal law in Delhi. His work covers drafting and arguing petitions, applications, complaints and appeals under the CPC, the BNSS and the BNS, before trial courts, the Delhi High Court and consumer forums.

He holds a master’s degree in criminal law, and criminal litigation remains the core of his practice alongside civil, family and consumer work.
